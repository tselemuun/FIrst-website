Moon flower

Ipomoea alba (aka. Moon flower) is a captivating night-blooming plant known for its large, white, trumpet-shaped blossoms that unfurl at dusk and glow softly under the moonlight. Its sweet, intoxicating fragrance fills the evening air, attracting nocturnal pollinators like moths. Often grown on trellises or fences, the vine can quickly cover spaces with its lush green leaves and elegant blooms. What makes the moonflower especially enchanting is its ephemeral beauty—each bloom lasts only for a single night, making its appearance feel magical and fleeting.

  • Every part of them is edible and they can be used in a variety of ways, including making tea, wine, and natural dye.
  • They can be difficult to remove because their strong, deep roots anchor them firmly in the soil.
  • They are perennial, which means they return and grow back year after year without needing to be replanted.
